Napkin prototypes

Three takes on the scribble pad and the reveal. All use the same Greggs question. Add ?demo=1 to prefill the pad, or ?end=1 to jump to the finished result.

A2. A real napkin, a tense reveal (redraft of A)A proper folded paper napkin on a light oak table: pressed emboss, quarter-fold creases, a wobbly edge, a pencil lying next to it, no ruled lines. The reveal is a countdown: the model writes its working line by line while a dot crawls along the ruler towards your ✗, then the vignette pulses, the total scrambles, lands, gets fact-checked, and the verdict slams down. About 12 seconds, skippable. Add ?fast=1 to see the returning-player pace. Now with a self-drawing doodle and crowd line, and a Share your napkin image showing only the question and your score. A. Pencil and odometerThe pad circles each number in red pencil as you write and builds your sum line by line. Reveal: the real answer spins up digit by digit, gets circled, and your guess drops onto a ruler. About 5 seconds. B. Chips and beer matGreen baize, a tilted napkin, and every number becomes a poker chip in a tray. Reveal: a beer mat hides the answer; tap to flip it off and your chip lands on the ruler. C. Live ruler and scratch cardA log ruler sits above the pad and your guess climbs it as you add numbers. Reveal: a scratch card; rub the foil off to uncover the answer yourself.

In every version the answer stays hidden until the moment, the model's working moves behind "How the model got there", and a Skip option is always there.
